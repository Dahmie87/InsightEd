Learning differences leave a digital fingerprint.

MindBridge reads that fingerprint — not through clinical tests, but through the natural patterns a child leaves when they simply use the app.

How It Works
01
Child learns normally
Stories, letter games, number puzzles. Nothing about the experience feels like a test.
02
Signals are logged
Every tap, hesitation, skip, error, and re-read is timestamped in the background.
03
Patterns emerge
The AI compares the child's behavioral fingerprint against known learning profiles.
04
App adapts silently
Pacing, format, and exercise type shift automatically for that specific child's brain.
05
Teacher is alerted
Plain language. No clinical jargon. Exactly what to do differently — and why.
The Three Conditions We Screen For
📚
Dyslexia
A phonological processing difference affecting 5–17% of children. The brain routes visual symbols differently — making decoding text effortful. Not a sign of low intelligence. Often the most intelligent children in the room.
Signals MindBridge Tracks
Letter reversal frequency (b/d, p/q, u/n)
Phonics hesitation vs. peer baseline
Re-read rate on same text passage
Word-skip and line-skip patterns
Oral vs. written comprehension divergence
⚡
ADHD
Affects executive function, impulse control, and sustained attention. Children are not naughty — their brain's attention circuitry is wired differently. Often mislabelled as laziness or behaviour problems.
Signals MindBridge Tracks
Attention drop-off curves over time
Impulsive selection (sub-1s on complex tasks)
High abandon rates mid-exercise
Inconsistent performance across sessions
Performance spikes on novel/stimulating content
🔢
Dyscalculia
A specific difficulty with numbers and mathematical concepts. Affects ~5% of children and is the most under-detected of the three conditions — especially in Africa, where maths failure is simply accepted.
Signals MindBridge Tracks
Digit reversal and transposition errors
Number magnitude comparison failures
Counting sequence irregularities
Spatial errors in written arithmetic layout
Strong verbal vs. weak symbolic math gap
Ethics & Safeguards
This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis
MindBridge flags children for teacher attention. It does not replace clinical assessment — it makes clinical referral possible where it was previously unimaginable.
No label until 60% confidence
Below this threshold, patterns are quietly logged but no flag is raised to the teacher. The system monitors, not judges.
Data stays on the device
All pattern analysis runs locally. No child's behavioral data is transmitted without explicit school consent.
Trained on African contexts
Models calibrated to African language phonology, Swahili and Hausa letter patterns, and local classroom pacing norms.
